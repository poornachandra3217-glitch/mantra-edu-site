// About Mantra

A summer school built around one belief.

Students learn fastest when they are building something that is actually theirs.

Our Story

Mantra Learning Academy began as a small summer workshop run by a handful of working developers who were frustrated with courses that stayed entirely inside slides. The first batch had twelve students and one shared goal: finish the summer with a live website, not just a certificate.

That first batch shipped their sites on time, and most of them kept building afterward. That result shaped everything that came after — smaller batches, more lab time, and a syllabus that treats deployment as seriously as design.

Our Mission

To give students a direct, honest path from "I want to learn to build websites" to "here is a website I built and deployed myself" — in a single summer, without unnecessary theory in the way.

What We Value

  • Working code over long lectures
  • Small batches over large lecture halls
  • Shipped projects over unfinished assignments
  • Honest feedback over easy grades

How We Teach

Our Approach

1. Show

Faculty demonstrate a real technique on a real project before students touch a keyboard.

2. Build

Students apply the same technique to their own project immediately, in the same session.

3. Review

Work is reviewed the same day, so mistakes are caught early instead of at the end of the course.
